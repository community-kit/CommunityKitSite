Overview

Six days of community events alongside WWDC

Meet the Community

Whether you're new to WWDC or a veteran, this is your home. Meet like-minded developers, designers, and creators from communities like iOSDevHappyHour, Hacking with Swift, try! Swift, and WWDCScholars.

Watch Parties

No ticket to Apple Park? No problem! Join us for our famous Keynote and State of the Union watch parties, where we'll be streaming all the action live with the best crowd in Cupertino.

Show Off Your Work

The indie dev community thrives on collaboration. We're hosting an Indie Fair where developers and designers of all backgrounds can show off their current project: an app, a design, or anything in between.

Live Entertainment

We're lining up live podcast recordings and workshops throughout the week, with something for everyone no matter your interests. More announcements coming soon, so follow us to be the first to know!
